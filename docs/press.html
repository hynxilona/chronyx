Press kit

Take the facts.
Leave the hype.

Everything below is safe to quote, and the disclaimer is part of the quote. If a detail is missing, open an issue with press: in the title.

01

Boilerplate

Three lengths. Copy the one that fits, keep the last sentence.

one line
Chronyx is a Rust blockchain written from scratch, where PoH orders, PoA finalizes, and PoW admits.
about 55 words
Chronyx is a from-scratch Rust blockchain: no framework, no VM, no token contract. Proof of History gives every node the same order at 50 ms ticks, Proof of Authority finalizes a block every 400 ms, and Proof of Work is how a validator earns a slot. The supply is fixed at 600,000,000. Public devnet, MIT licensed, not audited, no value.
about 130 words
Chronyx is a blockchain written from scratch in Rust, built to test one idea: ordering, finality and admission are three different jobs and should not be done by one mechanism. Proof of History is the clock — a sequential SHA-256 chain with a tick every 50 ms that proves time passed and gives every node the same order. Proof of Authority is the judge — four validators take round-robin slots and finalize a block every 400 ms with a quorum of three. Proof of Work is the door — joining the validator set costs hashcash-style work rather than a token purchase; the proof code exists, and wiring it into admission is the next milestone. The supply is fixed at 600,000,000 CHRONYX with no inflation and no mint path. It is a public devnet: not audited, no market, no value. MIT licensed. One maintainer.

04

Say this, not that

The difference is the whole credibility of the project.

Safe
  • A public devnet
  • Not audited, no value
  • Fixed supply of 600,000,000
  • PoH orders, PoA finalizes, PoW admits
  • MIT licensed, one maintainer
  • PoW admission is the next milestone
Do not write
  • Audited, or "audit in progress"
  • Mainnet, launch, or token generation event
  • Investment, opportunity, or price
  • Presale, whitelist, or allocation
  • Partnership with anyone who has not agreed
  • Listed, tradable, or "available on"

The disclaimer is the quote. “Devnet. Not audited, not mainnet, no value.” If an edit removes it, the piece is wrong even if every other sentence is right.
